# Interview notes — Product Engineering edition

## Strong discussion points

- Why the ranking requires an explicit decision lens rather than one universal score.
- How segmentation and saved decision context support repeat workflows.
- Why opportunity scoring is deterministic and transparent in a portfolio reference.
- How typed analytics connect decisions to activation, time-to-insight and reuse hypotheses.
- Why the Experiment Center distinguishes product learning from vanity metrics.
- How feature flags, explainability and saved research support progressive delivery.

## Trade-off to explain

The scoring model is a demonstrator, not a production recommendation engine. A real rollout would require user research, calibration, fairness review and outcome validation.
