# Product strategy

## Problem

Company research is rarely blocked by a lack of data; it is blocked by unclear decision context. Users can inspect dozens of metrics without knowing which evidence matters for the decision they are making.

## Product bet

Make decision context explicit before adding more data. CompanyScope Product asks the user to choose a lens and segment, then ranks the same evidence according to that declared intent.

## Core loop

1. Frame the decision with a lens.
2. Narrow the relevant segment.
3. Inspect a transparent priority ranking.
4. Save decision context for repeat work.
5. Open evidence and build a shortlist.
6. Measure whether the workflow improves activation, time to insight and reuse.

## Guardrail

Opportunity scores are deterministic demo heuristics. They are deliberately visible and testable. A production ranking would require validation, calibration, fairness review and user research before being presented as decision support.
